Opportunities

Curated, not exhaustive.

A working database of summer research programs, hospital volunteer programs, and science competitions open to high schoolers right now — with the real details: who's eligible, what it costs, and when it's due.

Program dates shift every year, and many organizations hadn't posted their 2027 cycle at the time this was written (August 2026). Every deadline below reflects the most recently confirmed cycle — always double-check the linked page before you build a plan around it.

Summer Programs

RESEARCH — DALLAS, TX

UT Southwestern STARS Summer Research Program

Eight weeks working side-by-side with a UT Southwestern faculty host in a real biomedical research lab, non-residential.

  • Eligibility Current juniors, 16+ by June 1, DFW-area only
  • Dates 8 weeks, June–Aug
  • Applications Nov 21 – Jan 20
  • Cost Free, paid stipend

EXPOSURE — DALLAS, TX

UT Southwestern HPREP

A lower-commitment entry point: workshops, mentoring sessions, and hands-on activities exposing DFW students to medicine and research.

  • Eligibility Grades 10–12, DFW area
  • Held Annually in January

RESEARCH — HOUSTON, TX

Baylor College of Medicine — SMS Summer Research Program

Eight weeks, 40 hrs/week in a BCM lab. Requires prior completion of Saturday Morning Science or an affiliate program — this isn't a cold-apply program.

  • Eligibility Mostly 18+ by June 1; some 16–17 spots
  • Dates June 8 – July 31
  • Deadline ~Mid-April
  • Cost Unpaid

EXPOSURE — HOUSTON, TX

Baylor College of Medicine — DocPrep

A week-long academic enrichment program exposing rising seniors from South Texas to a range of healthcare careers.

  • Eligibility Rising seniors, South Texas HS
  • Size ~50–60 students selected/year
  • Dates 1 week
Program page →
Highly Selective RESEARCH — STANFORD, CA

Stanford Institutes of Medicine Summer Research Program (SIMR)

Two months in a Stanford lab across 8 research tracks — cancer biology, neuroscience, bioengineering, and more — mentored by grad students and postdocs.

  • Eligibility Rising jr/sr, 16+ by start date
  • Dates ~8 weeks, June–July
  • Deadline ~Late February
  • Cost $50 app fee; aid available
  • Note Strong Bay Area preference, <3% acceptance
Program page →
RESEARCH — BETHESDA, MD + NATIONWIDE

NIH Summer Internship Program (SIP)

Federally run internships across every NIH institute — from cancer biology to bioinformatics — for students about to finish high school.

  • Eligibility Grad. seniors turning 18 by 9/30; US citizens/permanent residents
  • Applications Mid-Nov – mid-Feb
  • Cost Free, stipend paid
Program page →
RESEARCH — REMOTE / WORLDWIDE

Lumiere Research Scholar Program

One-on-one mentorship with a PhD researcher, fully remote — a good option if you don't have a local lab within reach yet.

  • Eligibility Grades 9–12, worldwide
  • Format ~12–15 weeks, 1-on-1
  • Deadline Rolling, Feb–July cohorts
  • Cost Paid program; need-based aid
Program page →
Highly Selective RESEARCH — NEW YORK, NY

Rockefeller University Summer Science Research Program

One of the most selective research placements in the country — about 32 scholars chosen per year to work in Rockefeller labs.

  • Eligibility Rising seniors, NYC-area
  • Deadline As early as January

Competitions

RESEARCH — GRADE 12 ONLY

Regeneron Science Talent Search (STS)

The country's oldest, most prestigious pre-college research competition — an individual project, not a group event.

  • Deadline ~Nov 5, 2026, 8pm ET
  • Selectivity ~2,000 → 300 Scholars → 40 Finalists
  • Top award $250,000
Official page →
RESEARCH — GRADES 9–12

Regeneron ISEF

You don't apply directly — you qualify by placing at a regional, ISEF-affiliated science fair first.

  • Regional deadlines Jan–Apr 2027
  • Finals May 8–14, 2027, Los Angeles
  • Scale 1,600+ students, 60+ countries
Official page →
EXAM — GRADES 9–12

USA Biology Olympiad (USABO)

A four-tier exam series testing biology depth well beyond AP Bio — school registers first, then students.

  • School registration ~Mid-Nov 2026

  • Fee $125/school
