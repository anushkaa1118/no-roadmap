About

Why I started this.

As the oldest child of immigrant parents, I learned early that many opportunities come with invisible instruction manuals.

People who already know the system know what questions to ask, who to email, and where to look. I usually did not. There was no older sibling showing me how to find research. No family blueprint for high school opportunities. No built-in understanding of internships, shadowing, summer programs, cold emailing, or what different careers in medicine actually looked like.

Over time, I learned by asking questions, sending emails, making mistakes, and finding mentors — mostly by trial and error. I realized that access is often not just about whether an opportunity exists. It's about whether someone knows that it exists, knows how to reach it, and has seen what the first step looks like.

No Roadmap is my attempt to make that process a little easier for the student who comes next.

You do not need to already know the path. Sometimes you just need to see how somebody else found theirs.
