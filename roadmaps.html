Every story ends the same way: with a path you can actually walk. Each roadmap turns one profile's experience into concrete first steps — what to learn, what to try, who to ask.

Roadmap 001

Exploring Radiology

Learn

Build the foundation before you need it.

  • Explore basic anatomy and medical imaging
  • Learn how X-ray, CT, MRI, and ultrasound differ

Watch

See what the field actually looks like.

  • Attend a virtual radiology lecture or case demonstration

Try

Get hands-on, even in a small way.

  • Complete a simple image-interpretation activity

Read

Go deeper on your own time.

  • Beginner resources recommended by a practicing radiologist

Explore

Find your way into the room.

  • Look for local radiology shadowing opportunities
  • Check hospital volunteer programs
  • Look into research involving medical imaging

Ask

Questions worth bringing to a mentor or shadowing day.

  • What does a radiologist actually do all day?
  • How much patient interaction is involved?
  • What kinds of students tend to enjoy radiology?
